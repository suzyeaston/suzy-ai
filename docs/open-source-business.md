# Open source and a future business

SUZY//AI can remain open source while becoming commercially useful.

Apache-2.0 allows people and companies to run, modify, and redistribute the core.

Potential paid layers later:

- hosted / managed SUZY//AI,
- easy desktop installer,
- synchronization and backup,
- collaboration,
- curated model packs,
- specialized creative tools,
- hardware controllers,
- Appliance objects,
- performances and installations,
- support / consulting,
- organizational deployments.

The business does not have to depend on hiding the basic intelligence architecture.

The private identity/data layer remains separate from the open core.
