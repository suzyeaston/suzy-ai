# Appliance integration direction

The Appliance Latent Space remains its own application and performance instrument.

Its existing eight-block musical timeline stays.

Its existing control vocabulary stays meaningful.

## Hardware direction

Preferred current hypothesis:

```text
physical toaster
    ↓ BLE
control events
    ↓
Appliance running on computer
    ↓
audio + visuals + optional SUZY//AI requests
```

The toaster can expose browning, destruction, latent axes, memory, plunge, capture, kill, and future controls without hosting a neural model.

## Important boundary

SUZY//AI does not own musical scheduling.

The Appliance does.

SUZY//AI may:

- receive musical events,
- remember approved examples,
- propose variations,
- provide model capabilities,
- connect performance events to a larger world timeline.

The instrument remains playable when the AI is unavailable.
