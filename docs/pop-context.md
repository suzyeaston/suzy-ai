# POP//CONTEXT integration direction

POP//CONTEXT remains the audiovisual observer.

Its job is to convert video/audio into timestamped evidence and, later, interpretation.

SUZY//AI supplies shared capabilities:

- model runtime,
- private memory,
- research tools,
- provenance,
- timeline/event storage.

POP//CONTEXT owns:

- video ingest,
- media segmentation,
- audiovisual evidence,
- its media timeline,
- interpretation UI.

A political or locally specific claim should be able to trigger a fresh research request rather than rely on frozen model knowledge.
