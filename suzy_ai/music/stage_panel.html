What the system can hear

It captures your selected Scarlett microphone input directly in RAM, measures approximate vocal notes, and sends those note observations and your chosen BPM/chord to Qwen on localhost.

Not yet connected: Logic's internal beat/MIDI bus, bar-clock synchronization or an expressive R&B instrument. The AI reply uses our basic synthesized note sound. This is a rehearsal panel, not a stage-ready mixer.

If anything sounds wrong, press STOP AI. Only the AI stream stops. Your Logic track keeps playing.
